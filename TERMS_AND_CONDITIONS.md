## 1. What UltraLite is

UltraLite is a free, ad-supported web browser optimised for low-bandwidth (2G / sub-64 kbps) Android networks. It includes:

- A **Normal mode** — full WebView browsing identical to other Android browsers.
- An **UltraLite mode** ("Pure Legacy") — server-side cleaned, text-first browsing for slow networks.
- A **Radio & Music** section that opens public broadcaster audio streams.
- A bundled set of bookmarks, downloads, history, and tab tools that operate entirely on your device.

UltraLite is **not** a search engine, social network, content host, or hosting service. UltraLite simply opens URLs you request and audio streams you tap. We do not host any third-party content.
